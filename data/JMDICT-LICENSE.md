# JMdict attribution

`data/jmdict-common.json` is derived from [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), the property of the [Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/), and is used in conformance with the Group's [licence](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html).

It is built from the "common words" English release of [jmdict-simplified](https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified) by `tools/build_jmdict.py`. It keeps up to three senses of up to three glosses per entry.
